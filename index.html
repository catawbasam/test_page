Outreach Resources for Local Meetings

You are not alone in your Advancement and Outreach efforts. The Advancement and Outreach Committee of Baltimore Yearly Meeting is here to help! On this page you will find some ideas and printed materials being used by other meetings. Where possible we have included editable versions of some of the printed material that can be downloaded and adapted for your purposes.

In addition to these samples, we made a special arrangement with Britain Yearly Meeting to make their Advancement and Outreach Handbook – 54 pages of ideas and how-tos available on this web site.

We are always looking for more examples, if you have an example you would like to share, e-mail it to: aando@bym-rsf.org. If you have specific A&O needs, please e-mail us a request and we will do our best to get you some help or resources.
